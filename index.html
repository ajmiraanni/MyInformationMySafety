My Information, My Safety!

Let's learn what information is safe to share and what information we should keep private.

Early primary Years • Ages 5-8
Watch & Learn Explore Play Quiz

👋 Welcome!

Today we are learning how to stay safe when we use computers, tablets and the internet. Some information is personal. Before sharing personal information online, stop and ask a trusted adult.

Learning goal: I can identify personal information and know when I should ask a trusted adult before sharing it online.

Step 1 · Watch & Learn

Watch the video

Listen for these three things

  1. 1Private information — things about me that are just for me and my family.
  2. 2Stop and think — before I type or tap, I stop.
  3. 3Tell a trusted grown-up — Mum, Dad, a carer or my teacher.

Keep these private

My full nameWhere I liveMy schoolMy birthdayPhone numberPasswordsPhotos of me
